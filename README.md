### CLIM-000246: Tidal seawater intrusion beneath grounded ice
- **Subfield:** Ice sheets, glaciers, and sea ice
- **Open:** Measure heat exchange where tides push seawater beneath grounded ice. Predict resulting melt and retreat while resolving cavities and freshwater drainage.
- **Status:** Rignot et al. (2024) observed widespread intrusions beneath Thwaites; converting geometry into validated melt laws remains open.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** May explain part of observed retreat underestimated by models.
- **Key works:** Rignot et al. (2024), [“Widespread seawater intrusions beneath the grounded ice of Thwaites Glacier, West Antarctica”](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2404766121).
- **Related:** Fluid-driven interface separation; turbulent heat transfer in cavities.
